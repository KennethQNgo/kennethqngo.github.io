## Background

Hello! I am Kenneth Ngo, a Spring '23 graduate from Cal State Fullerton with a B.S. degree in Biochemistry. In my free time, I explore computational chemistry and its applications in drug discovery. My academic background sparked a fascination with how computational tools can drive innovation in healthcare and improve patient outcomes.

## Focus

I have always been captivated by molecular interactions and the potential of computational methods to deepen our understanding of chemistry. As I consider furthering my education with a PhD in Computational Chemistry, I am eager to apply my skills and curiosity to challenging problems in the industry.
